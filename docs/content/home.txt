FAQs
1. What is an Amazon agency and how much does it cost to hire an Amazon agency?
An Amazon agency is an external team brought in for specialist marketplace work. Brands may use one for Amazon PPC, Amazon SEO, listing improvements, creative, or wider account management. Cost depends on what the agency is expected to own. A larger scope and heavier account workload generally require more support.
2. When should a brand hire an Amazon agency instead of managing Amazon in-house?
The tipping point often comes when Amazon begins competing with other priorities for staff time. A lack of specialist knowledge can create the same problem. Hiring an Amazon management agency lets your team retain strategic control while experienced external people manage defined areas of execution.
3. How do I choose the right Amazon agency for my brand?
Do not judge an agency by the length of its service list. Look at the people assigned to your account, the work they can prove, how they report progress, and how clearly they explain ownership. Experienced Amazon specialists should be visible before you sign.
4. What access does an Amazon agency need to my Seller Central account?
The permissions should follow the job. Someone managing advertising will need different Seller Central access from a team handling catalog management or listings. Give each external user the level of access their responsibility actually requires.
5. Who owns my Amazon account, campaigns, data and creative assets if I leave the agency?
Ownership becomes much easier when it is settled at the contracting stage. Include campaign history, Amazon account data, creative outputs, and editable source files in that discussion. Anything excluded from client ownership should be named clearly before the agency starts producing or managing assets.
6. Do Amazon agencies require long-term contracts?
A long commitment is not an automatic requirement. Different providers use different commercial terms. Before signing with an Amazon seller agency, identify the minimum term, renewal mechanism, cancellation notice, exit costs, and any obligations that continue after you stop using the service.
7. Can an Amazon agency take over an account that another agency currently manages?
Yes. The practical issue is not the takeover itself but the transfer of context. Your next Amazon marketplace agency should receive current access, campaign records, active tasks, recent performance information, and working documents. Better handovers reduce unnecessary rework during the first stage.
8. Can an Amazon agency work alongside our existing ecommerce or Amazon team?
It can. The agency can support the functions your current team does not want to own. Internal staff may keep product or commercial responsibilities. External Amazon experts can focus on specialist execution. The arrangement works best when responsibilities are agreed before day-to-day work starts
9. How long should we work with an Amazon agency before judging the results?
Not every part of Amazon moves at the same pace. Amazon advertising can produce useful signals relatively quickly, whereas Amazon SEO, listing optimization, and broader marketplace growth need enough traffic and sales data before the results mean much.
10. Can an Amazon agency manage brands across multiple Amazon marketplaces?
Possibly. Do not assume experience in one marketplace transfers everywhere. Ask about the countries involved, local advertising practices, language needs, Seller Central, Vendor Central, and Amazon FBA. International management should be supported by relevant operating experience.
