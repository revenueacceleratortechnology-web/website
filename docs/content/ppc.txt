FAQs
What does Amazon PPC management involve?
Amazon PPC management keeps campaigns from running unattended after setup. We manage the recurring choices around targets, bids, placements, budgets, and performance, with spend tied back to the products currently important to your business.
How do you manage my Amazon advertising budget?
Your budget is managed against evidence from the account. We look at spend quality, current product priorities, and campaign performance before reallocating money. Larger budget decisions remain visible to your team and require the approval structure agreed during scoping.
Which Amazon advertising formats can you manage?
We work across Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands, and eligible display formats. Not every account needs all three. Product priorities, targeting requirements, creative readiness, and current business needs determine which formats are worth using. DSP sits outside this scope.
How do you decide which keywords to target?
We look for terms that fit the product first. Search behavior, campaign evidence, and search-term reports then help us narrow the list. Match type is chosen according to how much reach or control the campaign needs.
What advertising reports will I receive?
The report is designed to explain performance rather than list activity. Spend, clicks, sales attribution, ACOS, ROAS, and relevant campaign changes are included. We also call out unresolved decisions and current account constraints.
Can you support branded advertising protection?
Yes, if branded protection is commercially useful for the account. That can include branded search coverage or relevant product-targeting work. Competition and product importance influence how much defensive activity is justified.
Can PPC data support work beyond advertising?
Yes. Advertising can expose shopper language, weak product-page response, or stronger-than-expected demand around certain ASINs. Those findings can feed into SEO, listing work, launches, and broader product prioritization.
What results can I expect from PPC management?
We do not assign the same expected result to every account. Budget, stock, demand, competition, listing quality, and campaign history all influence what paid media can deliver. Progress is judged against agreed account measures.
Can you help if I am new to Amazon advertising?
Yes. We can build from a blank or lightly used advertising account. Before campaign work begins, we review what you sell, where the priorities lie, how much budget is available, and what account data already exists.
How do you respond to changes in Amazon advertising?
Amazon changes its advertising tools over time. We assess those changes against the campaigns already running before doing anything. If an update creates a practical reason to adjust the account, we change the management approach accordingly.
