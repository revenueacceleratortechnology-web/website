FAQs
Who can access A+ Content?
Amazon requires a Professional selling account for A+ Content. Access also depends on having an eligible Brand Registry selling role or qualifying generic products within the catalog.
Does Amazon DSP require Amazon sales?
No. Amazon states that Amazon DSP is available to advertisers that sell through Amazon and advertisers that do not. This makes the platform relevant to broader programmatic advertising requirements beyond marketplace sales.
How does Remote Fulfillment handle inventory?
Remote Fulfillment can use eligible FBA stock already held within Amazon’s fulfillment network for orders placed through participating destination stores. Sellers do not need to position separate inventory in every supported market before making qualifying offers available.
Which products qualify for Remote Fulfillment?
Eligibility depends on Amazon’s Remote Fulfillment rules and a valid offer in the destination store. Trade requirements still apply. Restricted, prohibited, dangerous, or otherwise excluded products cannot be enrolled.
Is Vendor Central available to every seller?
No. Vendor Central is based on an invitation from Amazon. Vendors sell inventory wholesale to Amazon under a first-party arrangement. Seller Central follows a different model, with third-party businesses selling through Amazon’s store.
Can authorized resellers receive Brand Registry access?
Yes, where a brand administrator assigns the Reseller role. Amazon gives that authorized seller selected Brand Registry selling benefits, although access to certain sensitive information and tools remains restricted.
Can Brand Registry begin before Amazon selling?
Amazon states that intellectual-property owners with qualifying trademark rights can use Brand Registry even without selling products directly through the Amazon store. Individual selling benefits can still depend on the account and assigned role.
