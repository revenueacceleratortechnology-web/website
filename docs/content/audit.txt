Signs You May Need an Amazon Account Audit 
You do not need to diagnose the account before requesting an audit. Bring the performance problem you can already see. Our Amazon seller account audit follows the available evidence until the areas deserving deeper attention become identifiable.
Sales Slowdown
Flat or falling sales can originate across traffic, conversion, availability, advertising, or product performance. We examine the surrounding account signals before assigning the decline to one function.
Rising Ad Costs
Higher advertising costs can accompany weaker conversion, lower organic contribution, or changing product economics. An account-wide review establishes whether advertising deserves specialist investigation.
Weak Conversion
A product can receive plenty of visits and still underperform at the order stage. We inspect the offer, listing quality, product response, and other account conditions that may be weakening shopper progression.
Lost Visibility
Search visibility can slip because of indexing, inventory, catalog, or performance changes. The audit narrows down which signals need SEO attention and which ones belong with operations.
Catalog Friction
A suppressed listing or broken variation can affect sales long before it is noticed elsewhere. An Amazon Seller Central audit checks for these issues, along with incorrect attributes and stranded products.
Unclear Growth
Some accounts have no obvious failure but still lack a strong next move. RA Tech compares current performance and account constraints to locate opportunities worth investigating first.
